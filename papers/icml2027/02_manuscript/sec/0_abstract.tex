\begin{abstract}
Pruning spatial tokens at different layers of a 3D multimodal model changes both which tokens attention selects and when they are removed. On LLaVA-3D ScanQA validation, deleting each layer's top-ranked 10\% hurts exact match most relative to random deletion at L14 among three tested layers. Yet deleting the same L14-selected tokens at L8 rather than L14 costs a further 1.50 exact-match points. The layer that identifies important tokens need not be where their removal hurts most. We also test GeoGate, a training-free schedule that retains tokens ranked by FastV at L14 and compacts the prompt cache. On the ScanQA validation split used for selection, retaining 128 spatial tokens at L14 beats selected L2 and L8 controls by 1.22 and 1.03 exact-match points at matched analytical language-model cost. At 10\% retention, LLaVA-3D uses 42.1\% fewer analytical language-model FLOPs, but application time falls by only 0.8--1.4\% on one H100. The same schedule reduces 3D-LLaVA exact match by 0.95 points on SQA3D validation.
\end{abstract}
