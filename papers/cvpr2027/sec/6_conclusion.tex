\section{Conclusion and limitations}
GeoGate tests late spatial attention as a guide to training-free 3D token pruning. On LLaVA-3D ScanQA validation, its selected late schedule outperforms selected early controls at matched analytical LM cost. Fixed-set deletion shows depth effects without supporting an L14-specific damage maximum. Task-transfer losses and modest application-time gains limit the scope of this result. These findings motivate evaluating token relevance, retention quality, and measured cost separately. Independent quality preservation and a joint measured comparison with official 3DZip remain open.

The study covers two Llama-family 7B backbones and systems measurements on one H100. ScanQA informed selection, SQA3D test was previously inspected, and historical training exposure remains incompletely verified. Prompt perturbations do not establish arbitrary prompt robustness; set-level interventions resolve neither individual-token causality nor semantic correctness. Mixed-batch interval coverage and recovery of the historical quality runner remain unresolved. The supplement documents negative results, original aggregate plots, and eight fixed-rule answer examples.\label{maintext:end}
