\section{Related work}
\textbf{3D representations and compression.} 3D-LLM and PointLLM align spatial representations with language~\cite{hong20233dllm,xu2024pointllm}. Our testbeds contrast 3D-positioned image patches with compact superpoint tokens~\cite{zhu2024llava3d,deng20253dllava}. Recent compressors use spatial gating, learned or debiased importance, compensatory tokens, and semantic--geometric selection~\cite{wu2025adatoken3d,huang2025fast3d, lin2026efficient3d,zhang2026hcc3d,li2026segpruner,li2026geo3dpruner}. 3DZip combines spatial structure with feature diversity~\cite{baek2026zip}. Online geometry-aware pruning, CoverPrune, CoVeR, and HiSC emphasize coverage or clustering~\cite{lai2026seeing, ling2026coverprune,bui2026cover,qu2026hisc}. GeoGR combines spatial--semantic compression, recoverable group routing, and compression-aware post-training~\cite{zheng2026geogr}. Its late-entry and early-exit schedule is not an equivalent training-free control. We compare official 3DZip alongside scheduling controls that do not reproduce the corresponding official methods.

\textbf{Information flow and pruning depth.} FastV ranks visual tokens using last-prompt attention~\cite{chen2024fastv}. PyramidDrop exploits depth-dependent visual redundancy for progressive dropping~\cite{yuan2024pyramiddrop}, and HiMAP uses visual information flow to guide hierarchical pruning~\cite{yin2025himap}. AIM combines merging with in-model pruning~\cite{zhong2025aim}. GeoGate holds FastV's scorer fixed to study how last-prompt spatial attention informs pruning depth. Fixed-set controls examine whether deletion location explains the own-set L14 damage peak. Transfer and timing tests assess retention quality and practical cost; official 3DZip supplies an equal-token-count quality comparison.

\textbf{Attention and cache analysis.} Attention profiles describe model behavior but do not alone establish functional necessity~\cite{clark2019bertology,kovaleva2019bert}. StreamingLLM, H2O, SnapKV, and PyramidInfer optimize KV storage~\cite{xiao2024streamingllm,zhang2023h2o,li2024snapkv, yang2024pyramidinfer}. GeoGate couples prefill pruning with prompt-cache compaction, checked through position mapping and one-token decoding. The supplement details unavailable or incompatible baselines.
