\begin{abstract}
3D large multimodal models support question answering, object description, and language grounding, but processing their spatial tokens increases inference cost. Attention rankings identify pruning candidates, but their dependence on network depth complicates the choice of when to remove tokens. We propose GeoGate, a training-free schedule for late-layer token pruning motivated by renewed spatial attention. GeoGate retains the full sequence through block L14 and reuses FastV's last-prompt ranking to select spatial tokens without an extra scoring pass. It preserves text and original rotary positions, then compacts every layer's prompt key--value cache before decoding. We evaluate two Llama-family 7B models, with L14 and the default 10\% retention budget selected on ScanQA validation. On this split, LLaVA-3D with 128 retained spatial tokens improves exact match by 1.22/1.03 points over selected L2/L8 controls at matched analytical language-model (LM) cost. On SQA3D validation, 3D-LLaVA at 10\% retention yields a combined exact-match change of $-0.95$ points relative to the unpruned model. For LLaVA-3D at 10\% retention, GeoGate reduces analytical LM FLOPs by 42.1\% and initial prompt-cache storage by 78.5\%. Measured application time decreases by 0.8--1.4\% on one H100 GPU.
Code is available at~\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/GeoGate-E8AC/}.
\end{abstract}
